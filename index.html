Method and caveats

What comes from TM Forum

  • All 607 capabilities, with their domains, aspects, supertypes, identifiers and brief descriptions, come from GB1029C v4.0.0. TM Forum approved it for production on 22 May 2026.
  • 547 capabilities sit in the 72 domain-by-aspect blocks. The other 60 are composites (Domain, Strategy and Operations Management, plus the Commercial, Production and Business groupings).
  • The groupings follow TM Forum: Commercial is Market, Sales and Customer; Production is Product, Service and Resource; Business is Ecosystem and Enterprise.

What is our assessment

  • The IT solution areas, the IT dependency score (1 to 3) and the service fit score (0 to 3) are our own overlay, not TM Forum content. Validate them with practice leads before you use them with clients.
  • The placement of each reference on the map is our reading of its source.

Where the data lives

  • trends_data.json holds the trends; references_data.json holds the references and offerings. Editors can change both from this page: press Ctrl+Shift+E (Cmd+Shift+E on a Mac).
  • framework_data.json holds the TM Forum capabilities and our overlay. It is not edited from the page.

Gaps in the public evidence

  • We found no public case with a named Portuguese operator (NOS, MEO, Vodafone Portugal, DIGI).
  • We found no public reference for an Amdocs or Netcracker BSS programme, a named Salesforce Communications Cloud or ServiceNow TSM telco rollout, or a transformation described as ODA-based.
  • Most Iberian evidence is Telefónica Spain pilots and TM Forum Catalysts, plus MasOrange and Vodafone España.
  • Internal credentials will likely fill these gaps. Add them in edit mode.
About v4.0.1. We found no public listing or change log for a v4.0.1 pre-production release. Past point releases (3.0.1 to 3.0.5) made small sprint changes, so v4.0.0, the approved version, is the one to cite. When a member copy of v4.0.1 is available, we can compare it against this map.
